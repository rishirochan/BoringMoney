# Boring Money

## Register

product

## Users

People who want one private place to understand their finances without handing their data to another hosted service. They import bank statements, connect accounts with their own provider keys, and review transactions on a desktop computer.

## Product Purpose

Boring Money consolidates statement files and live financial accounts into a local-first personal finance dashboard. Users own the storage, provider accounts, and credentials. Success means they can inspect and query their money without depending on shared cloud infrastructure.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, practical, trustworthy. Copy is plain and specific. The product should feel like a dependable desktop utility, not a financial sales funnel.

## Anti-references

Avoid gamified budgeting, crypto-dashboard theatrics, bank marketing pages, and interfaces that hide where data or credentials are stored.

## Design Principles

- Keep data ownership visible.
- Explain sensitive actions before asking for credentials.
- Prefer familiar desktop controls and short task flows.
- Put financial detail ahead of decoration.
- Make every error recoverable and specific.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard use, focus visibility, form labeling, status announcements, and reduced motion. Do not rely on color alone to communicate account or connection state.
